The Cobra Story: Our Milton Keynes Club

We are a dynamic and dedicated group of passionate badminton players from Milton Keynes, united by a shared love for the exhilarating sport of badminton. Our journey struck its first shuttle in January 2025. Since then, we've consistently hit the courts at the National Badminton Centre (NBC) every Wednesday, fostering a friendly and social community built on sportsmanship, continuous skill enhancement, and pure enjoyment. We are an intermediate-level non-profit club focused on enjoyable yet competitive games, officially affiliated with Badminton England (CL008437) and proudly aligned with the 'A Sport that Cares' framework. The club is steered by a member-elected Cobra Council, with our newly elected board members and team captains taking up their roles on 1st June 2026 to keep pushing the club forward.

At our core, Cobra Smash is a fully volunteer-led non-profit. Every pound our members contribute goes straight back into the club: court hire at NBC, shuttles, league fees, equipment, and the social side of what we do. Nobody profits personally from Cobra Smash. Since 30 September 2026, club money has been held in a dedicated charitable club account in the club's name. Our Cobra Council gives their time freely, and every subscription is reinvested into keeping the sport welcoming, affordable, and well-run for everyone who walks through the doors. It's a simple idea, and one we're proud of: a club by members, for members.

We're also ahead of the curve when it comes to the game itself. From 3rd June 2026, every Cobra Smash club night has been played under the new BWF 3x15 scoring format, putting us a full seven months ahead of the international rollout in January 2027. Faster games, sharper rallies, and a little more pressure on every point are now part of our weekly routine.

Our regular sessions are a testament to our collective commitment to the game and to the strong bonds we've forged as a team.

Our Club's Ambitions

We're not just about friendly games; we have exciting plans to grow and elevate Cobra Smash Badminton Club to new heights...

  • League Success: In our debut MKDBA (Milton Keynes & District Badminton Association) season 2025-26, we finished top of Division 4 and earned promotion. For the 2026-27 season we field two teams, competing in Division 3 and Division 4. ⚡🏸
  • Climbing the Divisions: Our ambition for next season is to take both teams up a level, into Division 2 and Division 3.
  • Annual Club Events: Planning internal badminton tournaments and fantastic social events throughout the year.
  • Sharpening Our Fangs: Friendly matches with other local badminton groups to keep raising our level.

Since 30 September 2026 we've used a hybrid approach that keeps session payments simple to manage and fair for everyone.

Members: pay your share of the court cost for the session. Shuttles are already paid for through the £40 annual subscription.

Guests: pay your share of the court cost, plus a flat £3 for shuttles.

Leagues: league costs are separate from both, and shared only among the players in that league.

Payments go into the club's own HSBC UK Charitable Bank Account: a dedicated account in the club's name. We don't publish account details on the website — the Cobra Council shares them directly with members and guests.

Members enjoy priority access to all sessions and have the opportunity to represent the club in competitive leagues. This ensures you can play regularly and be part of our competitive teams if you wish.

Members also pay less on the night: the £40 annual subscription covers shuttles, so members pay only their share of the court cost, while guests add a flat £3 for shuttles.

League costs are separate from the annual subscription and from session payments, and are shared only among the participants in that specific league. The exact costs vary depending on the league and number of participants.

Our membership is currently full, but we regularly review availability based on member attendance and offer openings to people on our waiting list. In the meantime, we maintain the waiting list and invite guests to fill open slots according to our rotation policy.

Currently, Cobra Smash is not a coaching club and is not suitable for complete beginners. Our sessions are focused on social and competitive match play for experienced players. We recommend checking with the National Badminton Centre directly for any beginner coaching programs they may offer.

Partner With Us: Power the Cobras

Cobra Smash Badminton Club is a non-profit badminton club based in Milton Keynes, founded in January 2025 and run by a team of passionate players. We host weekly sessions year-round at the National Badminton Centre, with regular players and guest participants, offering competitive match play that provides consistent local visibility.

We compete in the Milton Keynes & District Badminton League (MKDBA) with two teams, in Divisions 3 and 4 for the 2026-27 season, providing exposure to 20+ local clubs and hundreds of league players. Our reach also continues to grow online through Instagram, Facebook, and our Latest Buzz updates, engaging the local badminton community.

Our non-profit model — a £40 annual member subscription alongside pay-as-you-play session costs — removes barriers to participation and ensures 100% of sponsorship support goes directly into shuttles, league fees, and player development, helping sustain grassroots badminton in Milton Keynes. All club funds, sponsorship included, are held in a dedicated charitable club account in the club's name.
