# CobraSmash Mission

CobraSmash is the automated digital hub and operational engine for the CobraSmash Badminton Club. The core goal is to provide frictionless management of club activities with zero deployment overhead. Starting with instant website updates for match victories, the platform will progressively scale to automate blog publishing, social media syndication (Facebook/Instagram), player event registrations with WhatsApp payment integration, and dynamic doubles-performance leaderboards.
